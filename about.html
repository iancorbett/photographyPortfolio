About the Artist

Celeste Corbett

A multidisciplinary artist exploring landscape, memory, observation, and the relationship between people and place.

Celeste works across painting, photography, and mixed-media practices, drawing inspiration from natural environments, everyday observations, and the visual character of the places around her.

Her work explores how color, texture, composition, and atmosphere can transform familiar subjects into something more reflective and personal.

Through both studio work and photography, she is interested in slowing down the act of looking and finding significance in details that might otherwise go unnoticed.
